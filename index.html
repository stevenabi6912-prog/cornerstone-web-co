Questions

The things people ask first.

What does "copy" mean, and who writes it?

Copy just means the words on your website — the sentences on your home page, the description of each service, the paragraph about how you got started.

On Starter, you send me those words. Most people paste them into an email, or I pull what's already on your old site or your Facebook page and tidy it up.

On Standard and Growth, I write them for you. We talk for about half an hour about what you do, who your customers are, and what you want people to understand — and I turn that into the actual page text. You read it and tell me what's wrong before anything goes live.

Most people find writing about their own business much harder than they expect. That's exactly why it's included above Starter.

Do I own my website?

Yes. You own the domain, your content, and the finished site. The domain is registered in your name, not mine.

If you ever decide to leave, I hand over the files and transfer the domain to you or whoever you've hired next. Nothing is held hostage. I'd rather you stay because the work is good than because leaving is painful.

What if I want to stop the care plan?

Cancel any time with 30 days' notice. There's no contract and no cancellation fee, and I'll help move the site to wherever you're going.

How long does it take?

The free mockup takes three to five days. After that, a Starter site is usually about a week, Standard is two to three weeks, and Growth depends on what it has to do.

The honest answer is that the biggest delay is almost always waiting on photos and details from the business owner — so the more you have ready, the faster it goes.

I already have a website. Can you fix it instead?

Usually I rebuild rather than patch. Most older small-business sites sit on a pile of themes and plugins that costs more to untangle than to replace, and you end up paying for archaeology instead of a website.

That said, I'll look at yours and tell you honestly. If it's worth keeping, I'll say so — even though that's a smaller job for me.

What do you actually need from me?

Less than you'd think. A conversation, whatever photos you already have, your hours and contact details, and a look at the draft when it's ready.

You don't need to know anything technical. You will never be asked to touch a DNS record, a registrar login, or a hosting control panel — that's the part I'm handling.

Do I have to pay everything up front?

No. The mockup is free. After you approve it, it's half to start and half when the site goes live. The care plan starts the month after launch.
